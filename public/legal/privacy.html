1. Who is responsible

The controller is [Full name], [Street and number], [ZIP City], Germany, email [Email]. See the legal notice. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require one for us.

2. The short version

  • You sign in with PayPal. We get your name, email, country and address from PayPal.
  • What you post (photo, description, price, reviews, profile picture) is public inside Homing.
  • Google Gemini looks at item photos and texts to check them and find buyers. It never gets your name, email or address.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • We only use storage on your device that Homing needs to work, so there is no cookie banner.

3. What we collect and why

GDPR = General Data Protection Regulation. “Art. 6(1)(b)” means we need it to give you the service you signed up for. “(c)” means a law requires it. “(f)” means a legitimate interest, which we name.

Your account (from “Log in with PayPal”)

Data
PayPal user ID, full name and first name, email, address (street, ZIP, city, country), when you joined.
Why
To create your account, keep deals inside your country, and put your address on orders and parcel labels. We don't send you marketing email.
Legal basis
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Who sees it
Others see only your first name, your profile picture, your country and when you joined. Your full name and address are shown only to the other side of a deal (see “Deals and shipping”).

The demo sign-in creates a made-up person with a made-up Berlin address. It holds no real data about you.

Everyone using Homing in your country, with your first name and profile picture. Don't show people, faces, house numbers or documents in item photos.

4. AI: Google Gemini

We use Google's Gemini API for three jobs:

  • Photo check: the item photo, title and description, to check that the photo really shows the item, and to note what can be seen and what the description is missing.
  • Matching: titles and descriptions of items, and the text of what people look for, to find which ones fit.
  • Packing tips: title, category and description of a sold item.

We send no names, emails, addresses or payment data to Google. Under Google's terms for the EEA, Google does not use this content to train or improve its products, and processes it as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. Google may keep it for a short time to detect abuse.

If the photo check says the photo doesn't show the item, the item is not posted. You can take a new photo. If you think the AI got it wrong, email us and a person will look at it.

Legal basis: art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the photo check and matching are part of Homing).

7. Cookies and storage on your device

Homing stores only what it needs to work:

homing_sid
Cookie. A random sign-in key, so you stay signed in. Our server keeps only a one-way hash of it, not the key itself. HttpOnly, lasts 30 days or until you sign out.
homing.user
Local storage. Your user ID, name, country and profile picture, so the app can show them at once while it checks your sign-in. It proves nothing on its own. Removed when you sign out.
homing.seen.…
Local storage. Which of your sales the app has already told you about, so “Sold!” shows only once.
homing.intro
Session storage. Remembers that the opening animation played. Gone when you close the tab.

All of these are strictly necessary for a service you asked for, so the law does not require consent for them (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German law that used to be called TTDSG). That is why Homing shows no cookie banner. We use no analytics, ads, tracking pixels or third-party cookies on Homing.

When you go to PayPal to sign in or pay, PayPal's own pages and cookies apply. These legal pages store nothing at all.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account: until you delete it.
  • Open items, searches, profile picture: until you delete them or your account.
  • Finished deals (order, addresses, payment IDs, tracking): 3 years after the end of the year of the deal, so problems and claims can be handled. Longer only if tax law requires it, at most 10 years.
  • Reviews and replies: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted too.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Reports: up to 1 year after we decided, longer if needed for legal proceedings.

  • have your data deleted (art. 17). Use “Delete my account” in “My stuff”, or email us;
  • restrict processing (art. 18);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (art. 21);
  • complain to a data protection authority (art. 77), for example the one in the German state where we are based: [Data protection authority of the operator's state, address, website].

Email [Email]. We answer within one month.

4. KI: Google Gemini

Wir nutzen die Gemini-API von Google für drei Aufgaben:

  • Fotoprüfung: Foto, Titel und Beschreibung eines Angebots, um zu prüfen, ob das Foto wirklich die Sache zeigt, und um festzuhalten, was zu sehen ist und was in der Beschreibung fehlt.
  • Treffer finden: Titel und Beschreibungen von Angeboten und der Text der Suchen, um zu sehen, was zusammenpasst.
  • Verpackungstipps: Titel, Kategorie und Beschreibung einer verkauften Sache.

Wir schicken keine Namen, E-Mails, Adressen oder Zahlungsdaten an Google. Nach Googles Bedingungen für den EWR nutzt Google diese Inhalte nicht zum Trainieren oder Verbessern seiner Produkte und verarbeitet sie als unser Auftragsverarbeiter nach seinem Data Processing Addendum. Google kann sie kurz speichern, um Missbrauch zu erkennen.

Sagt die Fotoprüfung, dass das Foto die Sache nicht zeigt, wird das Angebot nicht veröffentlicht. Du kannst ein neues Foto machen. Meinst du, die KI liegt falsch, schreib uns, dann prüft ein Mensch.

Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (Fotoprüfung und Treffer sind Teil von Homing).

7. Cookies und Speicher auf deinem Gerät

Homing speichert nur, was es zum Funktionieren braucht:

homing_sid
Cookie. Ein zufälliger Anmeldeschlüssel, damit du angemeldet bleibst. Unser Server speichert nur einen nicht umkehrbaren Hash davon, nicht den Schlüssel selbst. HttpOnly, gilt 30 Tage oder bis du dich abmeldest.

Local Storage. Deine Nutzer-ID, dein Name, Land und Profilbild, damit die App sie sofort zeigen kann, während sie deine Anmeldung prüft. Allein beweist das nichts. Wird beim Abmelden gelöscht.

Session Storage. Merkt sich, dass die Startanimation lief. Weg, sobald du den Tab schließt.

All das ist unbedingt erforderlich für einen Dienst, den du ausdrücklich nutzt. Dafür braucht es keine Einwilligung (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG, früher TTDSG). Deshalb zeigt Homing kein Cookie-Banner. Wir nutzen auf Homing keine Analyse-Tools, keine Werbung, keine Tracking-Pixel und keine Cookies von Dritten.

Wenn du zur Anmeldung oder Zahlung zu PayPal wechselst, gelten die Seiten und Cookies von PayPal. Diese Rechtsseiten speichern gar nichts.
